Why Printable Joy Lab?

Most printable sites offer fixed files: one size, one color, one start day. Here every template is generated on the spot from your settings – A4 or US Letter, Monday or Sunday, landscape or portrait, with your habits, categories and titles printed right on the page. Nothing is uploaded: everything is calculated in your browser.

The PDFs are true vector files. Lines stay razor-sharp, grids keep their exact spacing when printed at 100%, and the files stay small enough to email.

What you can make here

Paper. Lined paper in the three US school rulings – wide (11/32 in), college (9/32 in) and narrow (1/4 in) – or any custom spacing from 3 to 20 mm. Graph paper in metric and imperial grids with bold lines every 2, 4, 5 or 10 squares. Dot grid from 4 mm to 1 cm in three dot sizes, and isometric paper with 60° triangles as lines or dots.

Calendars. A monthly calendar for any month from 1900 to 2100 – or all twelve in one PDF – with ISO week numbers and a notes area, and a year at a glance on a single sheet. Both start the week on Monday or Sunday.

Planners. A weekly planner, dated or undated, with a to-do column; a daily planner with an hourly or half-hourly schedule you can set from 4:00 to midnight; and a meal planner with a grocery list sorted into five groups.

Trackers and lists. A habit tracker that prints your own habits, a checklist maker with up to 60 lines and optional pre-filled items, and a budget planner with planned, actual and difference columns for your own categories.

Made for real printers

Every template is built in the paper size you choose – A4, US Letter, A5 or Half Letter – so nothing has to be shrunk to fit. Grids, boxes and text stay at least 12 mm from the edge (9 mm on the two small sizes), more than the unprintable border of typical home printers; only the lines of lined paper deliberately run from edge to edge, and the small optional site credit sits in the bottom margin. The Print button sends the exact page size to your browser with no extra margins, and the PDF stores it too. Scale is the one setting to watch: print at 100% or “Actual size”, as our guide to printing at actual size explains, and a 5 mm grid really measures 5 mm.

Free, and how the site is paid for

All templates and guides are free to use, without an account. You may print the pages for yourself, your family, your classroom or your own business; please don't resell the files or offer them for download elsewhere. The site is financed by ads, which are labeled as such and never placed inside the generator. Analytics and personalized ads only run with your consent, which you can change at any time under “Privacy settings” at the bottom of every page.
