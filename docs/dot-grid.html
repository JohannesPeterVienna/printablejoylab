Dot grid paper

Structure without clutter: dots guide your lines, layouts and lettering, then step back so your writing stands out.

Why dot grid?

Dot grid paper sits between lined and blank. The dots give you a grid for straight lines, boxes and tables, but they are quiet enough that drawings and handwriting stay in the foreground. That flexibility made dot grid the standard paper for bullet journaling, and it is just as useful for meeting notes with small diagrams, lettering practice and quick wireframes. With 5 mm spacing, a portrait A4 page holds 38 × 54 dots, a US Letter page 39 × 51 and an A5 page 27 × 38.

Choosing the spacing and dot size

  • 5 mm is the spacing most dotted notebooks use. One row of handwriting usually takes the height of one dot gap, larger letters two.
  • 4 mm suits small handwriting and dense layouts such as a 31-day tracker grid.
  • 7 mm or 1 cm works well for lettering practice, children and big headings.
  • 1/4 inch matches US dotted notebooks and planner inserts.

Not sure? Print one page each of 4 mm and 5 mm and write a paragraph on both; the one where your handwriting looks natural is your spacing.

The dots come in three sizes: fine (about 0.36 mm across), medium (0.52 mm) and bold (0.72 mm). Fine dots almost vanish under ink; bold dots survive faint printers and photocopies. Medium gray is the default color because it stays visible without dominating.

How to lay out a page in three minutes

  1. Count dots instead of measuring: decide that a heading takes two rows and a box border sits one dot inside the edge.
  2. Draw only the outer lines with a ruler; the dots line up everything inside them.
  3. Leave one empty dot row between sections. It reads as clear spacing without wasting space.

A filled example: a monthly overview spread

On an A5 page with 5 mm dots, the month name goes in the top two rows. Below it, 31 rows on the left list the dates with weekday initials; that uses 31 of the 38 rows and leaves room for a short note at the bottom. On the right half of the page, a box 12 dots wide holds the month's tasks, and a smaller box below it collects birthdays. Each column of the date list is three dots wide: one for the date, one for a symbol, one for space before the text.

Bullet journal layouts to try

  • Future log: six boxes for the next six months, three per page.
  • Weekly spread: seven boxes of equal height plus a to-do column.
  • Tracker grid: one dot per day across, one row per habit.
  • Collections: books to read, gift ideas, packing lists.

Hand lettering on dots

Dots make consistent letter heights easy. On a 5 mm grid, a simple lettering layout uses two dot gaps (10 mm) for the x-height and one more gap above and below for ascenders and descenders, so one line of lettering takes four gaps. Mark the baseline with a light pencil stroke from dot to dot, keep the slant constant by aiming at a dot two rows up and one column across, and leave one empty gap between lines of text.

Paper, markers and ghosting

Two different things happen with markers. Ghosting is a shadow of the ink visible from the back; bleed-through means the ink actually soaks through the paper. Heavier, more opaque paper of 120 g/m² or more reduces both, and a spare sheet behind the page catches the rest. Test markers on a printed scrap first: some alcohol-based markers can smear inkjet ink, while laser toner is generally unaffected. In draft or toner-saving mode, fine dots may drop out completely, so print dot grid at normal quality.

Printing tips

To make an A5 booklet, print A5 pages directly, or print A4 pages and cut them in half. Heavier 100–120 g/m² paper reduces ghosting with markers. Keep the scale at 100% so the 5 mm dots really are 5 mm apart – otherwise pages printed on different days will not line up in a binder.

Questions

What size dots do bullet journals use?

Most dotted notebooks use a 5 mm grid. That is our default setting.

Will the dots show when I scan my notes?

Light, fine dots usually disappear or turn very faint in scans – one reason dot grid is popular for notes you want to digitize.

Which dot size works with watercolor or brush pens?

Fine dots in the light color. They are easy to see while you plan the layout and almost invisible once the page is painted or lettered.

Can I print dots on both sides?

Yes. The grid is centered on the page, so on most printers the dots on the front and back line up closely.

What is the difference between ghosting and bleed-through?

Ghosting is when you can see the writing from the back of the sheet; bleed-through is when the ink passes through and marks the next page. Thicker, more opaque paper helps with both.

Why do some dots disappear when I print?

Fine dots are only about 0.36 mm across. Draft and toner-saving modes, or a nearly empty cartridge, can skip them. Use normal print quality or the medium dot size.

Can I use the 1/4 inch spacing for US planner inserts?

Yes. 1/4 inch matches many US dotted notebooks and inserts; on Half Letter it gives 20 × 31 dots.
