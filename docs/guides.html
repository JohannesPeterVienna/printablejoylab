Guides

Practical know-how for printing, planning and paper.

These eight guides answer the questions that come up most often once you start printing your own planners, calendars and paper. Each one has concrete measurements, a worked example, a short FAQ and the standards it relies on, and links to the templates it talks about. Here is what each guide covers, so you can go straight to the one you need.

Which guide should you read?

Printing and paper

  • Your grid prints too small, or the bottom of your calendar is cut off? Start with How to print at actual size. It shows in numbers what “Fit to page” does (an A4 page fitted onto US Letter shrinks to about 94%), where the 100% setting hides in Chrome, Edge, Preview, Acrobat Reader and on phones, and how to check a print with a ruler.
  • Not sure which paper pattern or weight to print? Lined, grid, dot or isometric compares the four patterns with line and square counts for A4 and US Letter, converts paper weights between gsm and pounds, and plans a bedroom floor plan on graph paper step by step.

Calendars and planning

  • Printing a calendar for next year? Printable calendar 2027 explains ISO week numbers (week 1 of 2027 starts on January 4), lists the US federal holidays with their observed dates, and compares yearly, monthly and weekly layouts.
  • Want a weekly routine that holds? How to plan a week on paper walks through a 25-minute weekly review, a capacity check in hours and a fully filled example page.
  • Tired of the daily “What's for dinner?” Meal planning on one page is a timed 20-minute routine with a sorted grocery list and food-safe timing for leftovers.
  • Wondering whether planning on paper still makes sense? Paper or app? compares both honestly, summarizes what note-taking research does and does not show, and describes a hybrid routine.

Habits and journaling

  • Starting a habit tracker? Habit tracker ideas that actually stick turns vague goals into rows you can tick and shows how to review a finished month.
  • Curious about bullet journaling? The bullet journal starter kit covers rapid logging, the core collections, migration and a complete first month, set up page by page.

If you are new to printables, read the print guide first: the right scale setting makes the biggest difference to every template on this site.
