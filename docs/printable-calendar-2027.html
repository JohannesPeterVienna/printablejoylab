Printable calendar 2027: week numbers, holidays and layout choices

What is special about 2027, how ISO week numbers work, US federal holidays and which layout to print.

Reviewed November 3, 2026 · Printable Joy Lab editorial team

A calendar looks like the simplest printable there is – until you notice that January 1 is in “week 53”, that a holiday moved to a Friday, or that May needs six rows instead of five. This guide collects the facts about 2027 that matter when you print a calendar, and explains the layout choices in our calendar templates so you can pick the right one in a minute.

2027 at a glance

2027 is a common year with 365 days. It begins on a Friday and ends on a Friday. The next leap year is 2028, when February has 29 days again. A few consequences for printed calendars:

  • Week numbers: under ISO 8601, 2027 has 52 weeks. Week 1 starts on Monday, January 4. Friday, January 1 to Sunday, January 3 still belong to week 53 of 2026.
  • Six-row months: with a Monday start, May and August need six rows. With a Sunday start, January, May and October do. Six-row months have shorter day boxes, so they are worth checking before you print.
  • End of the year: week 52 of 2027 runs from Monday, December 27, 2027 to Sunday, January 2, 2028. Week 1 of 2028 begins on January 3, 2028.

How ISO week numbers work

ISO 8601 is the international standard for writing dates and times. Its week rules are short: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of the year. An equivalent rule is “the week that contains January 4”. A year therefore has 52 or 53 weeks. It has 53 when it starts on a Thursday, or when it is a leap year that starts on a Wednesday – 2026 is such a year, and the next one after that is 2032.

Week numbers are used in many workplaces, schools and supply chains, especially in Europe, because “delivery in week 14” is shorter than a date range. In the US they are less common, but they are useful for anything planned in weeks: training plans, school terms, sprints or the number of weeks until a due date.

In our monthly calendar, the small gray number at the left of each row is the ISO week. If you start the week on Sunday, each row runs Sunday to Saturday, while ISO weeks run Monday to Sunday; the number shown is the ISO week of the Monday in that row, which covers six of its seven days.

US federal holidays in 2027

Our calendars leave holidays blank, because they differ between countries, states and employers. If you are in the US, these are the federal holidays for 2027 to write in. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, federal employees normally observe it on the Friday before; when it falls on a Sunday, on the Monday after. Private employers and schools may handle this differently.

Monday or Sunday?

In the US, Canada and Japan, wall calendars traditionally start on Sunday; ISO 8601 and most European calendars start on Monday. Neither is more correct. A practical test: if your weekend plans usually span Saturday and Sunday, a Monday start keeps them together at the end of the row. If your week “begins” at church, with family on Sunday or with Sunday meal prep, a Sunday start may match your routine better. All our calendars offer both.

  1. Open the monthly calendar, set the year to 2027 and the month to All 12 months.
  2. Choose your paper size and landscape orientation; keep week numbers and the notes column switched on.
  3. Pick Sunday or Monday as the first day, and an accent color you like.
  4. Download the 12-page PDF and print it at 100% (see printing at actual size).
  5. Go through the holiday table above and write each observed date into its box, plus birthdays, school breaks and paydays.
  6. Print the yearly calendar for 2027 in the same accent color and use it as the cover page.

Punch the sheets for a binder or clip them to a board; with two-sided printing, each month's back stays free for notes.

FAQ

Why does my phone show a different week number?

Some apps use the US convention, in which week 1 is the week containing January 1 and weeks start on Sunday. ISO weeks start on Monday and week 1 contains January 4, so the two systems can differ by one.

Does 2027 have a week 53?

No. 2027 has 52 ISO weeks. The days January 1–3, 2027 belong to week 53 of 2026.

Can I print a calendar that starts in a month other than January?

Yes. Print single months one at a time – for example September 2027 to August 2028 for a school year.

Are holidays printed automatically?

No, the boxes stay blank so the calendar works in any country. Use the table above or your official national list.
