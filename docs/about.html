About Printable Joy Lab

Customisable printables, generated in your browser.

Printable Joy Lab is made by AdStern, a small digital agency in Vienna, Austria. We build free tools that are genuinely useful – and printables were a natural fit: almost everyone needs a planner page, a calendar or a sheet of graph paper now and then, but most downloads come in one fixed size and style.

How the templates work

Each template is described once as a set of lines, boxes and text positioned in millimetres. From that description your browser draws the live preview and creates the PDF – the same data for both, so what you see is what you print. Paper sizes follow ISO 216 (A4, A5) and the North American standards (US Letter, Half Letter). Calendars use the Gregorian calendar and ISO 8601 week numbers.

How we check them

Every time the site is updated, an automated test draws every template with many combinations of settings – all paper sizes, orientations, week starts and options – and checks that every line, box and label lands on the page. If a single check fails, the update is not published. We also print sample pages and measure them with a ruler.

Your data

Your settings, habits and lists never leave your device: the PDF is created in your browser, and the last settings are remembered in your browser's local storage. Ads and analytics only run with your consent – see the privacy policy.

Using the printables

You are welcome to print the pages for yourself, your family, your classroom or your own business. Please don't resell the files or offer them for download on other websites; link to the template page instead.
