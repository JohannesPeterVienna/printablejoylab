Hosting

This website is delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address, the requested address, browser information and time to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Fonts are hosted on our own server; no connection to Google Fonts is made.

Templates and PDF creation

All templates and PDFs are generated in your browser. Your settings, habits, lists and titles are not sent to us. To remember your last settings, we store them in your browser's local storage; this is necessary for the function you requested. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings. If you copy a share link, your settings are part of that link – only share it if the content is not private.

Consent management

For advertising and analytics we use Google's consent management solution “Privacy & messaging” (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. On your first visit you can accept, reject or choose individual purposes and vendors. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via “Privacy settings” in the footer.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We show ads via Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited). Google may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver ads, limit how often you see them and measure their performance. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); without consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

With your consent we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which templates are used and how the site can be improved. IP addresses are not stored in full by Google Analytics 4; Google signals and ad personalisation are disabled. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time via “Privacy settings”.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You can change or withdraw your consent to ads and analytics at any time yourself via “Privacy settings” at the bottom of every page.
