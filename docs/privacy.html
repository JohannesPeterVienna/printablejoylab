If you copy a share link, your settings are part of that link – only share it if the content is not private.

Interim consent notice for statistics (“Visitor tally”)

At the moment Google’s consent message is set up but is not displayed on this site. So that visits can still only be counted with your consent, we show our own small notice while this is the case. It appears about three seconds after a page has loaded if Google’s consent management has not reported a decision by then and does not report that the GDPR does not apply to your visit. It asks only about statistics with Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited); it is not a consent to advertising, and it never switches on any advertising storage or ad personalisation – these stay “denied”. “Accept” and “Decline” are equal choices; until you choose, Google Analytics stays off.

What is stored: your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under the key pjl-tally, with the content “granted” or “denied”, a version number and the time of your choice. This entry is necessary to respect your decision and is not sent to us or to Google. After 12 months, or if we change the notice, we ask again. If you accept, Google Analytics loads as described in the next section; it sets cookies on your device (_ga, _ga_…), and data may be transferred to the USA (Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Closing the notice with “×” (or Escape) stores nothing and is not consent; we ask again on the next page. If you decline or later withdraw, measurement stops at once, Google Analytics is switched off for this site and any Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_…) set for this domain are deleted.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and reading information on your device § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021, TKG 2021). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via “Privacy settings” at the bottom of every page, including this one. Priority of Google’s consent management: as soon as Google’s consent message is displayed or reports a decision, only that decision counts; our notice is then closed and no longer shown, and a choice stored in it is deleted; if you had accepted in our notice and then decline in Google’s message, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted as well. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics stays off and the notice is not shown. On pages without ads (such as this privacy policy and the imprint) no Google code is loaded; there “Privacy settings” opens this notice so you can change or withdraw a choice made in it.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We show ads via Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited). Google may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver ads, limit how often you see them and measure their performance. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Non-personalised ads, which are based on page content, still use cookies or similar identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention, so they also require your consent to storing and accessing information on your device. Without that consent Google can only show limited ads. Limited ads are not personalised and do not use cookies or local storage for personalised advertising; Google may still use technical data, and where permitted storage, for fraud prevention and frequency limits as described in its documentation. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we don’t request any ads: Google’s ad code still loads, but ad requests stay paused, and all Google storage and measurement features stay set to “denied”. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners show ads based on those visits. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of other participating vendors at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

With your consent we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which templates are used and how the site can be improved. Until you decide, all Google storage and measurement features in our setup are set to “denied” (Google Consent Mode). Analytics only loads once you have accepted it in our interim notice (see “Interim consent notice for statistics” above) while Google’s consent message is not displayed, or once Google’s consent message reports both your consent to “Store and/or access information on a device” for the vendor Google and your consent to analytics storage; if either is missing or unclear, it does not load. Where the consent message reports that the GDPR does not apply to your visit, analytics loads only if it reports analytics as granted or not requiring consent and confirms that you have not opted out under a U.S. state privacy law; otherwise it stays off. If you withdraw or change your consent, measurement stops immediately: Google Analytics is switched off and sends no further data until you consent again. IP addresses are not stored in full by Google Analytics 4; event data is stored for the retention period set in our Google Analytics property and then deleted; Google signals and ad personalisation are disabled. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time via “Privacy settings”.
