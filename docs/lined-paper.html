Lined paper

Choose the ruling, paper size and line color, then download a crisp vector PDF or print straight away.

Which ruling should you pick?

Lined paper is described by the distance between its lines. The three standard rulings come from US school paper, but they are used everywhere:

  • Wide ruled – 11/32 inch (8.7 mm). Generous space for larger handwriting, younger writers and anyone who finds tight lines tiring.
  • College ruled – 9/32 inch (7.1 mm). The most common choice for notes, letters and journals. It fits more text without feeling cramped.
  • Narrow ruled – 1/4 inch (6.35 mm). Popular with people who write small or want to squeeze a lot onto one page.

Need something else? Pick Custom and enter any spacing between 3 and 20 mm in half-millimeter steps. Many European exercise books use around 8–9 mm, while 6 mm suits compact note-taking. On a portrait A4 sheet with the header switched on, college ruling gives 35 writing lines; on US Letter it gives 33.

How to set up the page

  1. Paper size and orientation: choose the sheet that is in your printer. Portrait is normal for writing; landscape is useful for wide tables of words, such as vocabulary lists in two languages.
  2. Ruling: pick wide, college, narrow or a custom value.
  3. Margin line: the pale red vertical line sits 32 mm from the left edge on A4 and Letter (22 mm on A5 and Half Letter). It leaves a column for dates, question numbers or keywords.
  4. Header: adds a short Name and Date line at the top – useful for worksheets, exam practice and letters, unnecessary for a private journal.
  5. Line color: light gray, medium, dark, blue or your accent color.

A filled example: lecture notes

A student prints ten college-ruled A4 pages with margin and header. In the header go the course name and the date written as 2027-02-08, so the pages sort correctly later. During the lecture the main lines hold the notes in short sentences; the margin column is reserved for keywords such as “definition”, “exam!” or a question mark next to anything unclear. After class, the student writes a two-line summary on the last lines of the page. When revising, reading only the margin column and the summaries gives a quick overview of the whole lecture.

Light lines write better

Very dark lines compete with your handwriting. The light gray setting is the most pleasant for everyday writing; Blue gives the familiar notebook look, and Medium helps if your printer prints faintly. Light lines also fade more cleanly when you scan or photocopy finished notes.

Printing tips

The lines run all the way across the sheet; your printer simply leaves its own unprintable border white, so nothing important is lost. Keep the print scale at 100% so the spacing stays exactly as chosen – our guide to printing at actual size shows where that setting hides. Lined paper has no front or back, so for a notebook you can print the same page on both sides of each sheet. For fountain pens, 90–100 g/m² paper reduces show-through; see which paper for which job for weights in pounds and gsm.

Questions

Is college ruled the same as 7 mm lines?

Almost: college ruling is 9/32 inch, which is 7.14 mm. For most purposes the difference to 7 mm is invisible.

How do I make handwriting practice sheets?

Choose wide ruling or a custom spacing of 10–12 mm, switch the header on for the child's name, and use the medium line color so the lines stay visible.

Why does the margin line go from top to bottom?

It marks a full-height column, as in classic exercise paper. If you do not need it, untick “Red margin line” and the lines run uninterrupted.

Can I use the paper commercially?

You may print and use the pages for yourself, your family, your classroom or your own business. Please do not resell the files or offer them for download elsewhere.
