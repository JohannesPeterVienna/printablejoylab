Monthly calendar

A clean month grid with room to write. Pick the year and month – or all twelve months – and start the week on Monday or Sunday.

A calendar that fits your week

In the US, Canada and Japan, calendars traditionally start the week on Sunday. In most of Europe and under the international standard ISO 8601, the week starts on Monday. Choose whichever matches the way you plan: with a Monday start, Saturday and Sunday sit together at the end of each row; with a Sunday start, the weekend frames the week. Weekend boxes are lightly shaded and their dates appear in your accent color, so they stand out in either layout. Any year from 1900 to 2100 works, calculated with the Gregorian calendar.

Week numbers explained

The small gray numbers at the left edge are ISO 8601 week numbers: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of the year. That is why the first days of January sometimes belong to week 52 or 53 of the previous year – January 1–3, 2027, for example, are still in week 53 of 2026. With a Sunday start, the number shown belongs to the Monday in that row. Our 2027 calendar guide lists the week dates and the US federal holidays for the year.

How to make your calendar

  1. Enter the year and choose a single month or All 12 months for a 12-page PDF.
  2. Pick Monday or Sunday as the first day of the week.
  3. Choose the paper size and orientation. Landscape adds a 52 mm notes column on the right; portrait places a notes area below the grid.
  4. Switch week numbers and notes on or off, pick an accent color and a title style.

Months that span six rows – May 2027 in both layouts, for instance – get slightly shorter boxes. On A4 landscape a six-row month still gives day boxes of about 30 × 26 mm, enough for two or three short entries.

A filled example: a family calendar for one month

A family prints September in landscape and gives each person a pen color: blue for one parent's work trips, green for the other's late shifts, orange for the children's school and sports dates. Paydays are marked with a small “$”, the trash pickup with a dot in the corner of each Tuesday box. The notes column collects things that belong to the month but not to a day: the school supply list, the new soccer schedule, a reminder to book flu shots. The page hangs on the fridge, and whoever adds an appointment writes it straight into the box.

More ideas

  • Content or project calendar: plan posts, deadlines and launches with one color per channel or project.
  • Bill calendar: mark due dates for rent, subscriptions and insurance and tick them off when paid.
  • Training plan: write the planned distance in each box and the actual one next to it.

How big are the day boxes?

A month needs four, five or six rows, depending on the weekday it starts on, its length and your week start. February 2027 is a rare four-row month with a Monday start: February 1 is a Monday and the month has exactly 28 days. With a Sunday start it needs five rows. As a rule, one handwritten entry needs about 6–7 mm of height, so a 26 mm box holds three short lines and a 16 mm box on A5 one or two. If you write a lot into single days, portrait gives the tallest boxes; landscape gives the widest ones and the separate notes column.

Printing in black and white

The weekday bar is filled with your accent color and carries white lettering. In grayscale, light accents such as sand turn into a pale gray, and the white weekday names lose contrast. For black-and-white printers, choose the ink or mono accent; tomato, teal and plum also stay readable. Weekend boxes are tinted with a very light warm gray that usually survives grayscale printing as a faint shade.

More practical setups

  • Chore rotation: write one person's initials in the corner of each box, so the rota is visible to everyone.
  • Reusable month: slip a printed month into a sheet protector and write with a wet-erase marker; dry-erase markers smudge more easily when you touch the page.
  • Shift schedule: one letter per shift type – E, L, N – in the box, and a legend in the notes column.

Printing tips

For a wall calendar, print all twelve months double-sided so each month has a blank back, or single-sided on 120 g/m² paper so ink does not show through. Use the paper size that is in your tray – an A4 calendar printed on US Letter loses its bottom row unless the printer shrinks it. Need the whole year on one sheet? Use the yearly calendar.

Questions

Does the calendar include holidays?

No – holidays differ between countries and regions, so the day boxes are left free for you to fill in. US federal holidays for 2027 are listed in our 2027 calendar guide.

Which years can I create?

Any year from 1900 to 2100. All dates are calculated with the Gregorian calendar.

Can I print a school year from August to July?

Yes. Create the months one by one, changing the year when you reach January, and print them in order.

Why does my calendar show week 53?

Some years have 53 ISO weeks. 2026 is one of them, so its last week continues into the first days of January 2027.

Why does February 2027 have only four rows?

February 1, 2027 is a Monday and the month has 28 days, so with a Monday start it fills exactly four weeks. With a Sunday start, February 1 is the second column and the month spills into a fifth row.

Which accent color is best for a black-and-white printer?

Ink or mono. The weekday bar is filled with the accent color behind white text, and dark accents keep that text readable when printed in grayscale.

How much can I write in a day box?

On A4 or US Letter, two or three short entries in most months. Six-row months have the smallest boxes; if a month is busy, print it in portrait for taller boxes.
