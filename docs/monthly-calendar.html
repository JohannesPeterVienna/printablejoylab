Monthly calendar

A clean month grid with room to write. Pick the year and month – or all twelve months – and start the week on Monday or Sunday.

A calendar that fits your week

In the US, Canada and Japan, calendars traditionally start the week on Sunday. In most of Europe and under the international standard ISO 8601, the week starts on Monday. Choose whichever matches the way you plan – weekends then sit together at the end of each row.

Week numbers explained

Week numbers follow ISO 8601: week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of the year. That is why the first days of January sometimes belong to week 52 or 53 of the previous year. Week numbers are widely used in business, logistics and schools, so they are a handy addition to work calendars.

Twelve months in one file

Select All 12 months to create a complete year as a 12-page PDF – ready for a ring binder, a clipboard on the fridge or a wall calendar. Print it double-sided and every month will have a blank back for notes.

Ideas for using a monthly calendar

  • Family planner: give each person a colour for appointments, sports and school events.
  • Content or project calendar: plan posts, deadlines and launches at a glance.
  • Bill calendar: mark due dates for rent, subscriptions and insurance.
  • Meal theme calendar: pasta Tuesday, soup Friday – fewer daily decisions.

Landscape or portrait?

Landscape gives wider day boxes and a notes column on the right – best for desks and walls. Portrait fits binders and planners and moves the notes area below the grid.

Questions

Does the calendar include holidays?

No – holidays differ between countries and regions, so the day boxes are left free for you to fill in.

Which years can I create?

Any year from 1900 to 2100. All dates are calculated with the Gregorian calendar.
