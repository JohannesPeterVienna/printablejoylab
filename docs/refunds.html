Refund policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Our paid products are digital downloads (PDF files). Orders are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and handles payment, invoicing and sales tax.

Right of withdrawal for digital content

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you normally have a 14-day right of withdrawal. For digital content delivered immediately, this right ends once the download is provided, provided you agreed at checkout to immediate delivery and acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal.

Our promise

If a file is damaged, incomplete or not as described, write to office@adstern.com within 30 days of purchase with your order number. We will send a corrected file or refund your purchase in full. If you bought the wrong paper size or week start, don't worry – every download already contains all versions.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, it can take 5–10 business days to appear.
