Print test page

Print this page once, measure the square, and you know whether your printer keeps 5 mm at 5 mm – and how close to the edge it can print.

What the page measures

A print dialog can shrink a page without telling you. This sheet makes the result visible with three things you can measure:

  • A square of exactly 100 × 100 mm. Because it is 100 mm wide, the millimeters you measure are the print scale in percent: 94 mm means 94%.
  • A millimeter ruler and an inch ruler. Lay your own ruler next to them. If the two drift apart, the page was scaled. The rulers are 180 mm and 7 in long on A4, 190 mm and 7 in on US Letter, 130 mm and 5 in on A5, and 120 mm and 4 in on Half Letter.
  • Four edge rulers. In the middle of each side a short ruler starts exactly at the paper edge and runs 10 mm inwards (8 mm on A5 and Half Letter), with a tick every millimeter and a number at 5 and 10. Your printer cannot print the outermost part; the first tick that appears on paper is its blank border on that side.

Enter what you measured

Measure the printed square from the middle of one line to the middle of the opposite line, once across and once down, and type both numbers in. The check tells you the scale, what a 5 mm grid turns into, and which print setting produces exactly this result.

How to read the edge rulers

  1. Look at the ruler in the middle of the top edge. Find the tick with the number 5 and count back towards the edge until the ticks stop.
  2. If the last visible tick is the third one from the edge, the printer leaves 3 mm blank at the top.
  3. Repeat on the other three sides. Many printers have one side with a wider border than the others, so check all four.
  4. Compare with our templates: grids, boxes and text keep 12 mm from the edge on A4 and US Letter and 9 mm on A5 and Half Letter. If all your borders are smaller than that, every template prints completely at 100%.

If a ruler is missing entirely, the border on that side is wider than the ruler is long. In that case choose a larger paper size or check whether the printer is set to a different paper than the one in the tray.

When to print it again

The result belongs to one combination of program, printer and paper tray. Print the page again after you switch to another browser or PDF viewer, another printer or another paper size, and before a large print run such as a full year of calendar pages.

Questions

Why is the square 100 mm and not 10 cm or 4 inches?

It is the same length as 10 cm. With 100 mm the number you read on the ruler is the scale in percent without any calculation. The inch ruler on the page serves rulers without millimeters.

The square is 100 mm across but not down. What now?

A scale setting changes both directions by the same factor, so measure again first. If the difference stays above 1 mm, it does not come from a scaling option in the print dialog.

Can I switch the rulers off?

Yes. Both the inch ruler and the edge rulers have their own checkbox. The 100 mm square and the millimeter ruler are always printed.

Does the page work in landscape?

The page is always portrait. A scale setting applies to the whole sheet, so the result also holds for landscape pages printed with the same settings.

Which paper sizes can I test?

A4, US Letter, A5 and Half Letter – the four sizes in which every template on this site is made.
