Why Printable Joy Lab?

Most printable sites offer fixed files: one size, one colour, one start day. Here every template is generated on the spot from your settings – A4 or US Letter, Monday or Sunday, landscape or portrait, with your habits, categories and titles printed right on the page. Nothing is uploaded: everything is calculated in your browser.

The PDFs are true vector files. Lines stay razor-sharp, grids keep their exact spacing when printed at 100 %, and the files stay small enough to email.
