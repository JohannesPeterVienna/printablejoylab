Checklist & to-do list

From packing lists to project steps: give your list a title, choose the layout and pre-fill items if you want.

A checklist for every occasion

Checklists are one of the simplest tools for getting things done reliably. Pilots and surgeons use them to avoid forgetting critical steps – at home they save you from leaving the charger behind or missing an item on the moving-day list.

Ideas

  • Packing list for holidays, hospital bags or camping trips.
  • Moving checklist: utilities, address changes, keys.
  • Cleaning schedule with weekly and monthly tasks.
  • Project steps for renovations, events or product launches.
  • Reading or watch list, gift ideas and wish lists.

Pre-filled or blank?

Type items into the box (one per line) and they are printed on the lines – ideal for lists you reuse, like a weekly shopping list or a packing list. Leave it empty for a blank to-do list you fill in by hand.

Squares, circles or numbers?

Squares are the classic tick box. Circles look softer and are popular in bullet journals. Numbered lines work best for ranked lists and step-by-step instructions. The optional priority column adds three dots per line – fill in one, two or three to mark importance.

Make a better to-do list

  1. Write tasks as actions: “Call the plumber”, not “Plumber”.
  2. Split big tasks into steps that take less than an hour.
  3. Mark the three most important items and do them first.
  4. Move unfinished items to tomorrow's list deliberately – or delete them.

Questions

How many items fit on one page?

Up to 60 lines in two columns. With fewer lines, the spacing grows so the page still looks balanced.

Can I save my pre-filled list?

Your last settings are remembered in this browser. You can also copy a share link that contains your list.
